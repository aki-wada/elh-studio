IE-Map (cochlear skeleton for LUMEN)
The predefined cochlear shape that LUMEN fits to the fluid (the cochlear skeleton) is the centerline of the right-ear cochlear lumen (scala tympani, scala vestibuli and cupula) of IE-Map, extracted and resampled by tools/make_cochlear_skeleton.py (changes were made; the left ear uses its mirror image).
Ahmadi SA, Raiser TM, Ruehl RM, Flanagin VL, zu Eulenburg P. IE-Map: a novel in-vivo atlas and template of the human inner ear. Sci Rep 11, 3293 (2021). doi:10.1038/s41598-021-82716-0
IE-Map is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

VS-SEG (reference template for automatic localization)
The automatic-localization reference template in this file (vendor/atlas_vsseg_mean.json) is the average of six cases of the Vestibular-Schwannoma-SEG collection and their left-right mirrors, aligned on their inner-ear center points and sampled sparsely (changes were made). It is not the image of any single subject.
Shapey J, Kujawa A, Dorent R, Wang G, Bisdas S, Dimitriadis A, Grishchuck D, Paddick I, Kitchen N, Bradford R, Saeed S, Ourselin S, Vercauteren T. Segmentation of Vestibular Schwannoma from Magnetic Resonance Imaging: An Open Annotated Dataset and Baseline Algorithm (version 2) [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive (2021). https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.9YTJ-5Q73
The dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

IE-Vnet / IEVNet sample data (reproducibility materials only)
Source: https://github.com/pydsgz/IEVNet/tree/main/sample_data
Paper: Ahmadi SA et al. IE-Vnet: Deep Learning-Based Segmentation of the Inner Ear's Total Fluid Space. Front Neurol (2022). https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2022.663200
License: Apache License 2.0 (LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt).
The original SPACE MRI crops are not included. The small reference_annotations.npz
contains new cochlear/vestibular and canal/common-crus label masks drawn or derived
from those public sample images. Changes: manual anatomical partition, grid mapping
and a one-voxel rim rule; no original MRI intensity array is in this NPZ.
Synthetic outputs generated locally retain source-derived anatomy and MRC signal.
They must not be described as having no patient-derived content.
